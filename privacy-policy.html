2. Data protection principles

In relation to personal data, we will:

  • process it fairly, lawfully and in a clear, transparent way;
  • collect personal data only for specific and legitimate reasons;
  • only use it in the way that we have told you about;
  • ensure it is correct and up to date;
  • keep your data for only as long as we require it;
  • process it in a way designed to ensure it is not lost, destroyed or used for anything you are not aware of.

  • Your name and contact details (email, phone, address) when you book an event ticket, join the Community, subscribe to our newsletter, or contact us;

  • Payment-related information for event tickets — this is processed by our third-party ticketing provider, Ticket Tailor; The Pearl Boardroom does not directly receive or store your card details;

4. How we collect your data

We collect data about you when you:

As set out in our Terms and Conditions, we regularly take photographs and video at our Events for use on our website, social media and in our marketing. We do this on the basis of our legitimate interests in promoting The Pearl Boardroom, having weighed this against your rights. If you would prefer not to be photographed, please let a member of the team know on arrival so we can exclude you, and contact us at anastasia@anastria.com if you would like a specific image removed after the fact.

8. Why we process your data

The Pearl Boardroom can only process your data for specific purposes permitted by GDPR and Cyprus data protection law:

9. If you do not provide your data to us

If you do not provide the data we need, we may not be able to process your Event booking, Community membership, or respond to your enquiry.

We will never sell your personal data to any third party. We share data only where necessary to provide our services.

Some of these providers — including Stripe and our website host — may process your data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where this happens, we require appropriate safeguards to be in place, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or a valid adequacy decision, so that your data continues to receive an equivalent level of protection.

11. Protecting your data

We take appropriate technical and organisational steps to protect your data against accidental loss, disclosure, destruction or misuse, including limiting access to those who need it, using appropriately secure systems, and requiring written agreements with any third party that processes data on our behalf.

The internet is not a completely secure method of communication, so we cannot guarantee the absolute security of information sent to us online, though we take great care to protect it.

12. How long we keep your data

We keep your data only for as long as necessary for the purpose it was collected. You can ask us to erase your data at any time, in writing where possible, and we conduct periodic reviews to delete data we no longer need.

13. Automated decision making

No decision with a significant impact on you will ever be made about you solely on the basis of automated decision-making, without human involvement.

14. Your rights in respect of your data

GDPR and Cyprus data protection law give you the following rights, in summary:

Cookies Policy

What is a cookie?

A cookie is a piece of data sent from a website and stored on your device. When you visit our website again, the data stored in the cookie can be retrieved to recognise your previous activity.

You must have cookies enabled to book tickets, join the Community, or contact us through our website.

Our cookies do not store personal information such as your name, contact number, email or postal address, but may allow us to access this information once you are logged in or contacting us via our website.

Our website uses cookies to:

  • identify you when you visit our website or contact us through it;
  • remember your preferences and past activity on our site;
  • allow you to share our content on your own social media platforms;
  • improve our website, content and services;
  • offer you a better experience with The Pearl Boardroom.

How can you manage your cookies?

To review, limit, block or delete cookies from our website, use your browser settings to manage cookie preferences.
